GABOUT THE PROJECT

Meet GeoMark.

GeoMark is a learning prototype exploring how location-aware verification and digital records could support campus attendance workflows.

◎

Our goal

Make attendance records easier to manage and review for students and faculty.

⚙

Our approach

Build in small stages: interface, API, authentication, database, validation, and testing.

Project status

This is a frontend-only prototype. It has no real user accounts or persistent attendance records.
